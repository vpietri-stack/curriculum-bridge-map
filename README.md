# Curriculum Bridge Map

An interactive, standalone teaching map between iShow 2.0, Power Up 1–3, and the revised PEP English curriculum (three-year starting point). Search by unit/topic, theme, or grammar focus. The PEP catalog currently covers Grade 3 upper through Grade 6 upper.

## Sources and interpretation

- iShow and Power Up: the teacher's TLS workbooks. Published data uses condensed teaching aims, vocabulary words, and derived grammar labels; the workbooks are not redistributed.
- PEP unit titles and links: [ai2book 点读宝](https://www.ai2book.com/diandu/), checked 12 Nov 2026.
- Edition structure: [People's Education Press overview](https://www.pep.com.cn/xw/zt/hd/12/xjcjs/xx/202409/t20240920_1995564.html).

Matches are theme-based suggestions. The suggested bridge language and activities are original planning ideas, not assertions about exact PEP assessment targets. Check the linked textbook pages for details. The currently listed Grade 6 lower book belongs to an older sequence and is excluded.

## Local use

Run `python -m http.server 8000` in this directory, then open `http://localhost:8000`. The site has no build dependencies. `build_data.py` regenerates `data.json` when the teacher's local TLS files are present.
