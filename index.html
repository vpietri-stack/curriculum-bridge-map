How this map is built

Teaching source
iShow A3/P1–P4 and Power Up 1–3 TLS workbooks supplied by the teacher. Unit language is condensed from the workbook for browsing.

Public school source
Revised PEP English, three-year starting point, Grade 3 upper through Grade 6 upper, from ai2book. The publisher’s overview confirms the revised structure.

Interpretation
Connections are suggested by shared themes and language in the TLS. Bridge activities are teaching suggestions, not claims about exact PEP assessed vocabulary or grammar. Check the linked PEP pages before planning an exam lesson.

Edition boundary · As of 12 Nov 2026, ai2book lists the revised series through Grade 6 upper. Grade 6 lower is excluded because the listed book is from the previous sequence. Textbook art, passages and audio are not reproduced here.
